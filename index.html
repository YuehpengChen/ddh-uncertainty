Method details

Stage 1 learns landmark configurations and coordinate variances from repeated annotations. Stage 2 predicts the learned representation from an image alone.

Repeated stochastic predictions describe changes between landmark configurations. The joint coordinate distribution is propagated through the α and β geometry shown above. Coordinates that vary together can produce different angle uncertainty from coordinates that vary independently.

Limitations & privacy

“90%” is the nominal central interval, not a guarantee of coverage for a new patient. The α and β intervals require separate calibration and independent clinical evaluation.

Landmark prediction does not establish an adequate Graf measurement plane. Image quality, anatomy and the remaining clinical criteria require expert review. This demonstration is for research use only.
