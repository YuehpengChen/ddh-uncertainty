02 / The research idea

Keep the landmarks connected.

The measurement depends on how points move together.

Stage 1 learns landmark configurations and coordinate variances from repeated annotations. Stage 2 predicts the learned representation from an image alone.

Repeated stochastic predictions describe changes between landmark configurations. The resulting joint coordinate distribution is propagated through the same α and β geometry shown above.

03 / Read the output responsibly

A research interface,
not a clinical decision.

Intervals need evaluation.

“90%” is the nominal central interval, not a guarantee of coverage for a new patient. The α and β intervals require separate calibration and independent clinical evaluation.

The image plane matters.

Landmark prediction does not establish an adequate Graf measurement plane. Image quality, anatomy and the remaining clinical criteria require expert review.

Local means local.

Your image is decoded and processed in this browser. There are no analytics, image uploads or remote inference calls. The model and web assets are downloaded from the published hosting service.
